Our Work

Building sustainable beekeeping across Malawi through cooperative development, training, and market access

Strategic Pillars

Our approach is built on three interconnected pillars that strengthen beekeeping communities across Malawi.

🐝

Cooperative Development

Forming and strengthening self-sustainable beekeeping cooperatives, groups, and individual farmer networks across districts.

🌱

Sustainable Production

Training beekeepers in modern, environmentally-friendly production techniques while protecting both bees and natural resources.

🛍️

Market Access

Creating reliable market pathways for honey and bee products, connecting producers with buyers and expanding domestic and export opportunities.

Active Initiatives

Current programmes driving impact across Malawi's beekeeping communities.

District Cooperative Expansion

Active

Establishing new cooperative branches and strengthening existing ones across Nkhata-Bay, Mzuzu, Karonga, and Kasungu districts. Focus on capacity building and operational sustainability.

Region: Northern & Central Malawi

Focus: Cooperative formation, governance, training

Youth in Beekeeping Programme

Active

Engaging young people (18-35) in beekeeping as a viable livelihood. Includes training, equipment support, and mentorship to build youth participation in the sector.

Target: 500+ youth across regions

Focus: Skills training, business development, market linkage

Women Empowerment & Economic Inclusion

Active

Creating pathways for women to participate fully in beekeeping value chains. Includes training in production, processing, and business management with special focus on product diversification.

Target: 300+ women participating

Focus: Beekeeping skills, financial literacy, leadership

Sustainable Production & Environmental Protection

Active

Training programmes on sustainable beekeeping practices that protect bee health and environmental integrity. Covers modern hive management, pest control, and forest conservation.

Region: All cooperative districts

Focus: Environmental sustainability, climate adaptation

Value Addition & Market Development

Upcoming

Developing processing and packaging infrastructure to add value to honey and bee products. Includes training in food safety, product standards, and brand development for market competitiveness.

Focus: Product innovation, quality standards, branding

Timeline: Q3 2026

How We Work

Our integrated approach ensures sustainable impact at every stage.

1

Mobilize & Train

We identify and mobilize beekeeping communities, providing foundational training in modern techniques, cooperative principles, and sustainable practices.

2

Support & Monitor

Ongoing technical support, equipment provision through grants, financial services access, and regular monitoring ensure cooperatives remain strong and productive.

3

Market & Grow

We connect producers with reliable markets, facilitate value addition, and support business growth so beekeepers achieve sustainable livelihoods and prosperity.
