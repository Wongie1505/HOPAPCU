Who We Are

Leading Sustainable Beekeeping
Across Malawi

The Honey Producers & Processors Cooperative Union aims to become a world-class producer, processor, and marketer of specialty certified honeybee products, utilizing sustainable cooperatives and modern bee farming systems to improve livelihoods for beekeepers across Malawi.

Why Join HOPAPCU

What We Do For Our Members

01

Market Access

We connect members to reliable local and international markets, buying honey products directly at competitive cash prices.

02

Training & Equipment

Members receive practical skills training, access to modern beekeeping equipment, and tools through grants.

03

Financial Support

From cooperative loans to year-end dividends and bonuses, HOPAPCU ensures members share in the success they help create.
