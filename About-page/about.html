About HOPAPCU

Leading sustainable beekeeping and cooperative enterprise across Malawi

Our Vision

We aspire to be leaders in achieving a better quality of life for Malawian Beekeepers through sustainable Beekeeping and cooperative initiatives.

Our Mission

The Honey Producers & Processors Cooperative Union aims to become world trade producers, processors, and marketers of specialty and certified Honeybee products, utilizing sustainable cooperatives and Honey-based Bee farming new systems.

Our Objectives

  1. Promote the formation of self-sustainable bee farmers cooperatives, groups and individuals in Malawi.
  2. Foster the production of honeybee products using sustainable technologies and facilitate access to finance for producers, cooperatives, groups, individuals and the Union.
  3. Expand the processing and marketing of Honeybee products and other products on both domestic and international markets.
  4. Develop a strong voice to promote the interests of its members and to contribute to the development of natural resources and beekeeping in Malawi.
  5. Enhance women's, youth, disabilities and old age persons participation at all levels of the Beekeeping business. Encourage business diversification at the local bee farmer, cooperative, and union levels to ensure self-sustaining businesses.

HOPAPCU Community

Governance Structure & Functions

General Assembly / Conference

The General Assembly (Conference) is the supreme body of the Union, which meets once every two to three years or as stipulated by the HOPAPCU constitution. It deliberates on board policies of the organisation in the form of resolutions and empowers the national board of Directors committee to pursue them within a specified time frame. The Assembly examines the past activities of the Union and plans for the future, discusses the audited financial reports, and elects national officers including the Union President, Vice President, Secretary, Assistant Secretary General, Treasurer General, audit committee members, and members of the National Board of Directors committee.

Board of Directors

The Board of Directors is composed of elected officers and representatives of the various primary cooperatives within the Union. Between conferences, the Board runs the Union and delegates powers to sub-committees including Education, Finance, Audit, Procurement, Production, and Market committees. These sub-committees report to the Board of Directors on their activities at quarterly or six-monthly meetings. The Board executes the organisation's policies as decided by the General Assembly and has the constitutional right to initiate projects aligned with HOPAPCU's objectives, which are included in its report to the general conference.

Sub-Committees

Education Committee

Responsible for planning and execution of educational programs for cooperative members and leadership, with a view to strengthening the organisation.

Finance Committee

Supervises the income and expenditure of the union and ensures proper utilisation of union funds. Helps the Board prepare annual budgets and ensures the Secretariat prepares trial balances at the end of each year.

Audit Committee

Ensures financial accountability and transparency in all union operations and member transactions.

Procurement Committee

Manages purchasing and supply chain operations for union projects and member support.

Production Committee

Oversees quality standards and best practices in honey production and beekeeping across member cooperatives.

Market Committee

Develops market access strategies, manages buyer relationships, and expands sales channels for member products.

Service Charter

Our commitment to member service

HOPAPCU commits to providing fair and transparent services to all members. We pledge to uphold the highest standards of integrity, ensure equitable treatment, provide regular communication on cooperative affairs, and work towards the prosperity of all members through sustainable beekeeping practices.

Transparency & Accountability

Operating with integrity

As a cooperative union, we operate with full transparency and accountability to our members. We hold regular General Assemblies, publish audited financial reports, and maintain open communication channels. Every member has the right to information about cooperative operations and performance.

Annual Reports

Our performance and progress

We publish comprehensive annual reports detailing our activities, financial performance, member statistics, and impact across Malawi. These reports are presented and discussed at our Annual General Meetings.
