About HOPAPCU

Leading sustainable beekeeping and cooperative enterprise across Malawi

Our Vision

We aspire to be leaders in achieving a better quality of life for Malawian Beekeepers through sustainable Beekeeping and cooperative initiatives.

Our Mission

The Honey Producers & Processors Cooperative Union aims to become world trade producers, processors, and marketers of specialty and certified Honeybee products, utilizing sustainable cooperatives and Honey-based Bee farming new systems.

Our Objectives

  1. Promote the formation of self-sustainable bee farmers cooperatives, groups and individuals in Malawi.
  2. Foster the production of honeybee products using sustainable technologies and facilitate access to finance for producers, cooperatives, groups, individuals and the Union.
  3. Expand the processing and marketing of Honeybee products and other products on both domestic and international markets.
  4. Develop a strong voice to promote the interests of its members and to contribute to the development of natural resources and beekeeping in Malawi.
  5. Enhance women's, youth, disabilities and old age persons participation at all levels of the Beekeeping business. Encourage business diversification at the local bee farmer, cooperative, and union levels to ensure self-sustaining businesses.

Membership Structure

HOPAPCU is open to beekeeping groups, cooperatives, companies, and individual beekeepers. Here's our membership structure and fee schedule:

Member Type Entry Fee Annual Fee Share Limit
Primary Cooperative K50,000 K35,000 Up to 25 shares
Beekeeping Club/Group K50,000 K35,000 20 shares
Individual Beekeeper K35,000 K25,000 10 shares
Beekeeping Company K70,000 K50,000 15 shares

Each share costs K50,000. Members receive annual dividends and bonuses based on the volume of business conducted with the union.

Service Charter

Our commitment to member service

HOPAPCU commits to providing fair and transparent services to all members. We pledge to uphold the highest standards of integrity, ensure equitable treatment, provide regular communication on cooperative affairs, and work towards the prosperity of all members through sustainable beekeeping practices.

Transparency & Accountability

Operating with integrity

As a cooperative union, we operate with full transparency and accountability to our members. We hold regular General Assemblies, publish audited financial reports, and maintain open communication channels. Every member has the right to information about cooperative operations and performance.

Annual Reports

Our performance and progress

We publish comprehensive annual reports detailing our activities, financial performance, member statistics, and impact across Malawi. These reports are presented and discussed at our Annual General Meetings.

Annual reports available upon request from our office

Our Location

Based in Nkhata-Bay, Malawi, HOPAPCU is centrally positioned to serve beekeeping communities across the northern and central regions. Our office at the FAAD Malawi Building provides coordination, support services, and a meeting point for members.
