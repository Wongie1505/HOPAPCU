Our Vision:

  We aspire to be leaders in achieving a better

quality of life for Malawian Beekeepers through
sustainable Beekeeping  and cooperative initia-
tives.

Our Mission:
  The Honey Producers & Processors Cooperative
Union (HPAPCU) aims to become world trade
producers, processors, and marketers of specialty
and certified Honeybee products, utilizing sustain-
able cooperatives and Honey-based Bee farming
new systems.

Objectives:
1.  Promote the formation of self-sustainable bee
farmers cooperatives, groups and individuals in
Malawi.

2.  Foster the production of honeybee products using
sustainable technologies  and facilitate access to
finance for producers, cooperatives, groups, indi-
viduals and  the Union.

3.  Expand the processing and marketing of Honey-
bee products and other  products on both domes-
tic Markets and international markets.

4.  To develop a strong voice to promote the inter-

ests of its members and to  contribute to the de-
velopment natural resources and beekeeping in
Malawi.

5.  Enhance women’s, youth, disabilities and old age
persons participation at all  levels of the Beekeep-
ing business.  Encourage business diversification
at the local bee farmer, cooperative, and  union
levels to ensure self-sustaining businesses.

CORE VALUES

2. Ownership

5. Transparency and Accountability
