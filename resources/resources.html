Activities & Events

Recent training sessions, workshops, and collaborative initiatives across HOPAPCU's network.

September 2026

Women Beekeeping Training - Nkhata-Bay

Location: Nkhata-Bay District

Intensive three-day training programme teaching women beekeepers modern hive management, bee handling safety, and sustainable production practices. Facilitated by HOPAPCU technical staff.

August 2026

Value Addition Workshop Series

Location: Mzuzu

Two-week workshop on product diversification: beeswax processing, cosmetics production, mead making, and medicinal bee product applications. Hands-on demonstrations and business costing included.

July 2026

Cooperative Leadership Forum

Location: Lilongwe

Forum bringing together cooperative leaders to share best practices, discuss governance challenges, and plan collaborative marketing strategies for member cooperatives.
