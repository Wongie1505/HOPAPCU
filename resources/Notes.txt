Based on our discussion, here is the proposed structure for your website. I have organized everything you shared into 7 clear sections so visitors can find what they need quickly. Please review and let me know if anything needs to be added, removed, or changed before I start building."

1. HOME
The first page every visitor sees. It will have your group photo as the main image, a short introduction to HOPAPCU, three key benefits of joining or working with you, key statistics (members, districts, year founded), recent news preview, and a strip showing your partners. Two main buttons: Join the Cooperative and Visit Marketplace.

2. ABOUT US
Everything about the organization in one place, organized into three sections visitors can navigate:

Who We Are: your identity, vision, mission, values, and cooperative principles
Leadership: governance structure, board of directors, executive management, and secretariat
Accountability: service charter, annual reports, transparency statement, and partner affiliations

3. PROGRAMMES
Showcases what HOPAPCU actually does on the ground — activities, projects, initiatives, and your strategic plan for 2026 to 2030. This is where donors and government partners will look to understand your impact.

4. MEMBERSHIP
Explains how to join, membership types and fees, benefits of being a member, and shareholder information. Clear step-by-step process so anyone can understand how to become part of HOPAPCU.

5. MARKETPLACE
Where visitors can see your honey products and contact you to buy. Simple product showcase with photos, descriptions, and a contact-to-order button. No online payment system at this stage.

6. NEWS & ACTIVITIES
All your events, meetings, ambassador visits, training sessions, and photos in one place. This keeps the site feeling active and current.

7. CONTACT
Address, phone numbers, email, contact form, and map showing your location in Nkhata-Bay.
