Membership

Join a community of sustainable beekeepers and unlock access to markets, training, and growth opportunities

How to Join HOPAPCU

Membership is open to all beekeeping groups, cooperatives, and individuals. Follow these simple steps to become part of our union.

01

Complete Application

Fill out the membership application form. Contact us to request a form or visit our office at FAAD Malawi Building, Mkondezi.

02

Pay Entry Fee

Submit your application with the required entry fee based on your membership type (see fees table below).

03

Confirmation

Once your application is reviewed and accepted, you'll receive confirmation and your membership certificate.

04

Start Benefiting

Access reliable markets, training programs, equipment grants, and connect with other beekeepers in the network.

Membership Types & Fees

Choose the membership type that fits your situation. All fees are in Malawian Kwacha (MK).

Member Type Entry Fee Annual Fee Share Limit
Primary Cooperative K50,000 K35,000 Up to 25

Entry fees are one-time payments upon joining. Annual fees are due each year to maintain your membership status.

Why Join HOPAPCU?

Members gain access to market opportunities, support services, and a network of like-minded beekeepers.

🛍️

Reliable Market Access

HOPAPCU provides guaranteed market for your honey and bee products. We buy directly from members at competitive cash prices.

💰

Fair Pricing

Receive good prices for your products paid in cash. No middlemen, no delays. Direct access to buyers through the union.

📚

Training & Skills

Access practical beekeeping training programs to improve your techniques, productivity, and product quality.

🔧

Equipment & Tools

Receive beekeeping equipment and tools through grants. Invest in quality gear to grow your operation.

💵

Financial Services

Access loans and financing to expand your beekeeping business. Grow at your own pace with union support.

🎁

Dividends & Bonuses

Members receive year-end dividends and bonuses based on business volume conducted with the union.

Become a Shareholder

Members can purchase shares to increase their stake and voting power in HOPAPCU. Each share costs K50,000.

Primary Cooperatives

Up to 25 shares

Primary cooperatives can hold the maximum number of shares to strengthen their investment and influence in HOPAPCU's decisions.

What shareholding means:

  • Increased voting rights in cooperative decisions
  • Priority access to equipment and training programs
  • Higher dividend and bonus payouts based on shareholding percentage

Ready to Join?

Get in touch with us to start your membership journey. We're here to answer questions and guide you through the process.
