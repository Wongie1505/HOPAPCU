Frequently Asked Questions

What is HOPAPCU?

HOPAPCU is the Honey Producers & Processors Cooperative Union of Malawi. We bring together beekeeping cooperatives, groups, and individual beekeepers to strengthen production, access markets, and build sustainable livelihoods.

How do I join HOPAPCU?

Visit our Membership page to learn about membership types and fees. You can also contact us directly and we'll guide you through the registration process.

Do you offer training?

Yes! We run regular training programmes on beekeeping techniques, business management, and sustainable production. Contact us to ask about upcoming workshops in your district.

How can I buy HOPAPCU honey and products?

Visit our Marketplace to browse and order honey, beeswax, and beekeeping equipment directly from our members. We deliver across Malawi.

What are your response times?

We aim to reply to all emails within 24 business hours. For urgent matters, please call us directly.

Are you open to partnerships?

Yes! We welcome partnerships with NGOs, government agencies, and organizations supporting beekeeping development. Use the contact form to tell us about your interest.
